# Recovery assessment

The currently working stock-custom firmware and canonical stock image remain
archived locally as recovery references. No recovery mode has been triggered.

For this exact board/CFE, emergency HTTP/TFTP, reset sequence, accepted image
format, and safe exit are not yet proven. Community reports or a recovery flow
for another BCM63168 board are not sufficient. Because there is no UART, first
installation remains prohibited until non-UART recovery and stock rollback are
evidenced for this exact device and image writer.
